Get up and running

Verba, step by step

01

Record and refine

Allow microphone and speech-recognition access, then tap Record. Keep the text as spoken, clean it up, make it professional, create an email or message, turn it into notes, or summarize it. The selected mode is highlighted in teal; choose More for the complete list.

02

Translate a transcript

Choose Translate after recording. Your two preferred languages from Settings appear first; choose More Languages for the full list. Translation and other writing transformations use Verba’s local model.

03

Work with Notes

Create, edit, dictate into, copy, share, pin, tag, and refine notes. Verba keeps revisions so you can review earlier versions, while search helps you find notes by text, tag, or date.

04

Search by date

Search Notes and History with words or dates. Try today, yesterday, a month or year, or filters such as from:2026-08-01, after:2026-08-01, and before:2026-09-01.

05

Manage history

History is organized into Today, Yesterday, dates, months, and years. Each dictation shows a concise title, duration, and word count. In Settings, keep history for one day, one week, one month, one year, or forever—or delete it manually.

06

Build your dictionary

Review suggested uncommon words, names, and terms before adding them. Verba filters ordinary words and never adds a suggestion without your approval. Search and manage accepted words from the Dictionary tab.

07

Install the keyboard

Go to iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose Verba. Enable Full Access so the keyboard can privately exchange commands and finished text with the Verba app.

08

Dictate from another app

Select the Verba keyboard in Messages, Mail, Signal, WhatsApp, Slack, or another text field. If Verba is not already ready, iOS may open the main app to activate recording. Return with the iOS app switcher or the system Back control, then dictate from the keyboard.

09

Keep Verba ready

In Verba Settings, enable Keep Verba ready. iOS shows its microphone indicator while standby is active. Standby audio is discarded and is not transcribed, saved, or used for suggestions.

10

Install the local model

The optional Gemma writing model is stored on your device and requires several gigabytes of free space. Wi-Fi is recommended. Verba verifies the model before installing it privately.

11

Choose your appearance

Open Settings → Appearance and choose System, Light, or Dark. Verba uses high-contrast text, controls, icons, and teal selection states in every appearance.

12

Something isn’t working

Confirm that Microphone and Speech Recognition are enabled, Siri & Dictation is on, and the Verba keyboard has Full Access. Reopen Verba and resume Keep Verba ready if iOS suspended it. For more help, use Settings → Help & Feedback.

Local-first by design

Your words stay under your control.

Verba has no advertising, behavioral analytics, or account requirement. Your optional model, transcripts, notes, tags, history, dictionary, and writing preferences remain in Verba’s local app storage unless you choose to share or copy something.

Help & feedback

Still need help?

Email support@mogabr.com, or open Verba → Settings → Help & Feedback. You can optionally include app and device details or a recent diagnostic activity log. Both options are user-controlled, and Verba never automatically attaches recordings, transcripts, notes, or finished text.
