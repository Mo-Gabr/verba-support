Get up and running

Verba, step by step

01

Start dictating

Open Verba, allow microphone and speech-recognition access, then tap Record. Speak naturally and tap Stop when you are finished.

02

Ask Tim

Choose what Tim should do—clean it up, make it professional, create an email, organize notes, summarize, or make a message—then tap Ask Tim.

03

Use the Verba keyboard

In iOS Settings, go to General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose Verba. Enable Full Access so the keyboard can privately communicate with the Verba app.

04

Keep Verba ready

In Verba Settings, enable Keep Verba ready. iOS will show its microphone indicator while standby is active. The keyboard can then start and finish dictation without a floating PiP window.

05

Install the local writing model

The optional Gemma writing model is stored on your device and requires several gigabytes of free space. Wi-Fi is recommended. Verba verifies the model before installing it privately.

06

Something isn’t working

Confirm that Microphone and Speech Recognition are enabled for Verba, Siri & Dictation are enabled, and the Verba keyboard has Full Access. Reopen Verba and resume Keep Verba ready if iOS suspended it.

Local-first by design

Your words stay under your control.

Verba has no advertising, behavioral analytics, or Verba account requirement. Your optional Gemma model, history, and dictionary live on your device.
