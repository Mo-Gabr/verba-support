Verba is a private, local-first, voice-powered writing assistant.

Visit Support →
01

The short version

Verba is designed to minimize data collection. We do not operate advertising, behavioral analytics, cross-app tracking, or user-account services. Verba’s transcripts, notes, history, dictionary, and writing preferences are stored locally on your device.

02

Microphone and speech recognition

Verba requests microphone and speech-recognition permission so it can turn your speech into text. On supported devices, Verba uses Apple’s on-device speech technologies and installed speech assets. On devices or operating-system versions where on-device recognition is unavailable, Apple’s speech-recognition services may process audio under Apple’s privacy terms.

Verba does not send recordings or transcripts to a Verba-operated server.

03

Keep Verba ready

If you enable Keep Verba ready, Verba maintains a user-controlled microphone standby session so its keyboard can request dictation while you are writing in another app. iOS displays a microphone indicator while this session is active. Standby audio is not transcribed or uploaded. A temporary local audio file may be created to maintain the session and is deleted when standby stops or restarts.

04

Local writing model

Verba can use a Gemma writing model stored locally on your device to transform transcripts. The model is not included in the initial app download because of its size. When Verba downloads the model, it connects to the model host, currently Hugging Face. That provider may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

After installation, Verba’s Gemma writing transformations run on your device. The model and its cache remain inside Verba’s private app storage unless you delete the app or remove the model.

05

Notes, history, dictionary, and preferences

If history is enabled, Verba stores transcript text, dates, duration, word counts, and locally generated titles on your device. Notes, tags, pinned state, and note revisions are also stored locally. Search—including text and date filters—runs against this local content.

Dictionary terms, suggested terms, observation counts, and rejected suggestions remain local. Suggested words require your approval before they are added. Verba may also store your selected writing modes, translation-language preferences, appearance setting, and a limited set of local edit examples used to personalize writing results.

If Auto-copy finished text is enabled, Verba places the processed result on the iOS clipboard so you can paste it. Other apps may be able to read clipboard contents according to iOS controls.

06

Keyboard extension

The Verba keyboard uses an Apple App Group to exchange commands and finished text with the Verba app. Full Access is required for this private connection. Verba does not use Full Access to profile what you type, sell keyboard activity, operate advertising, or track you across apps.

Apple does not allow a custom keyboard extension to capture microphone audio directly. When Verba is not ready, the keyboard may ask iOS to open the Verba app so recording can be activated. iOS controls app switching and may require you to return through the app switcher or system Back control.

07

Data sharing and sale

Verba does not sell personal information. We do not share dictations for advertising or cross-app tracking. Information may be disclosed only when required by law or necessary to protect users, rights, and service integrity.

08

Retention and deletion

You can set History to be deleted after one day, one week, one month, or one year, or keep it until you delete it. You can also delete individual items or all history manually. Notes and dictionary entries remain until you delete them. Removing Verba deletes its locally stored app data; model removal deletes the downloaded local model. Apple speech assets are managed by iOS.

09

Help and feedback

When you use Settings → Help & Feedback, Verba shares only the information you choose through the iOS share sheet. You may optionally include an app and device summary. Verba does not automatically attach recordings, transcripts, notes, history, dictionary entries, or keyboard activity.

10

Children’s privacy

Verba is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Verba-operated service.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Verba changes. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

12

Contact

For privacy or support questions, open Verba → Settings → Help & Feedback. You can also review troubleshooting guidance on the Verba Support page.
