Privacy Policy

Verba is a private, local-first voice writing assistant. This policy explains the current iOS app and keyboard.

Visit Support →

01

The short version

Verba does not operate advertising, behavioral analytics, cross-app tracking, or user accounts. Transcripts, notes, conversation data, history, dictionary entries, and writing preferences are stored locally on your device.

02

Microphone and speech recognition

Verba requests microphone and speech-recognition access to convert speech into text. On supported devices it uses Apple’s on-device speech technologies and installed speech assets. If on-device recognition is unavailable, Apple’s speech-recognition services may process audio under Apple’s terms.

Verba does not send recordings or transcripts to a Verba-operated server. If you start a dictation session for the Verba keyboard, Verba keeps the microphone open in the background until the session ends, so iOS shows its microphone indicator and a Live Activity the whole time. Audio received while you are not dictating is discarded immediately in memory; it is never recorded to a file, transcribed, or saved. Only speech between tapping the keyboard microphone and tapping it again is transcribed. You can end the session at any time from the Live Activity or the app.

03

Keyboard extension

With Full Access off, the Verba extension remains a typing keyboard. With Full Access on, the keyboard and main app use an Apple App Group to exchange a pending command, live status, and the finished text you requested. Full Access is not used to profile typing, sell activity, serve advertising, or track you across apps.

Apple does not permit custom keyboard extensions to capture microphone audio, so the keyboard never uses the microphone itself. Keyboard dictation is recorded and processed by the main Verba app during a dictation session you start. The keyboard can open Verba to start a session. The finished text is inserted once and is not kept for reuse; a short operational log of keyboard and session events, which never contains your words, stays on your device unless you share it with support.

04

Local writing models

Supported devices may use Apple Intelligence. You may also download Verba’s optional Gemma 4 local writing model. The download host, currently Hugging Face, receives ordinary network information such as your IP address under its own policy. Once installed, text transformations run on your device and the model remains inside Verba’s app storage until removed.

05

Conversation Mode and speaker models

Conversation Mode records audio in Verba, creates a transcript, and uses optional FluidAudio speaker-identification models to group transcript segments by speaker. The model host may receive ordinary download network information. Speaker analysis runs locally after download.

Conversation audio, timestamps, speaker labels, and transcript text remain in Verba’s Application Support storage. The audio is deleted with its note, when replaced, or if analysis fails.

06

Notes, history, dictionary, and preferences

If history is enabled, Verba stores transcript text, dates, durations, word counts, and locally generated titles. Notes may include tags, pins, revisions, conversation speakers, and retained conversation audio. Search and date filtering run locally.

Personal dictionary terms, suggestions, observation counts, selected modes, translation preferences, appearance, and limited local before-and-after writing examples remain on your device. Suggestions require your approval.

07

Clipboard and sharing

If Auto-copy is enabled, Verba places finished text on the iOS clipboard. Other apps may access clipboard content according to iOS controls. When you copy, share, export, or submit feedback, information leaves Verba only through the destination and options you choose.

08

Help, feedback, and diagnostics

Help & Feedback uses the iOS share sheet. You may optionally include app/device details and a bounded operational log of recent app and keyboard state transitions. The diagnostic log does not contain recordings, transcript text, notes, or finished dictation text. Nothing is sent automatically.

09

Data sale and tracking

Verba does not sell personal information, share dictations for advertising, or track users across other companies’ apps and websites.

10

Retention and deletion

History can be disabled, deleted manually, or automatically deleted after the selected period. Notes and dictionary entries remain until deleted. Removing an optional model deletes that model. Deleting Verba removes its locally stored app data; Apple-managed speech assets are controlled by iOS.

11

Children and policy changes

Verba is not directed to children under 13, and no Verba-operated account service knowingly collects information from children. This policy may change as Verba develops; the effective date will change when material revisions are published.

12

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email support@mogabr.com or visit Verba Support.
